Welcome to Music App Manager

Your personal dashboard for music production apps and plugins.

1
Scan for Apps

Go to Tools → App Scanner and click Scan for Music Apps. The app reads your /Applications folder and auto-detects music software, identifying the manufacturer from each app's bundle.

2
Scan for Plugins

Click Scan for Plugins to catalogue your VST, VST3, and AU plugins from all standard directories. Plugins are added to your library in one click.

3
Launch & Manage

Click any app card to launch it instantly. Use Edit Mode to drag cards into your preferred order, or use the search bar to find anything fast.
