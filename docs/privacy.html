Effective Date: October 2026 • © Unforgettable Management Ltd 2026. All rights reserved.

1. Local-First Data Storage

Music App Manager, published by Unforgettable Management Ltd, is designed with a local-first architecture. All application catalogs, plugin inventories, custom categories, Studio Session presets, ratings, personal notes, and information entered into the License & Serial Key Vault are stored locally on your computer inside your user application data directory (apps-config.json).

  • We do not upload or transmit your serial numbers, license keys, or local file paths to any external server.
  • You retain full control over your local configuration file and can export or back it up at any time using the Export Config JSON tool.

2. Advertising, Sponsorships & Partner Content

To keep Music App Manager free for music producers, audio engineers, and musicians, the Software may be funded by sponsorships, partner placements, affiliate links, or advertising.

  • Sponsor & Ad Delivery: Where sponsor banners, featured audio tool recommendations, or promotional links are displayed within the Software, fetching those creative assets or clicking external partner links may connect your device to third-party sponsor websites or ad delivery servers, which may log standard technical metadata (such as your IP address, operating system type, or referral click) in accordance with their own privacy policies.
  • External Links: Clicking a sponsor link, manufacturer link, or affiliate link will open the destination in your default web browser. Unforgettable Management Ltd is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external third-party websites.

3. Security of Locally Stored License Information

Any serial keys, activation codes, or account email addresses you choose to enter into the License, Serial Key & Account Vault are stored in plain JSON format on your local hard drive so that you can view, copy, and export them easily.

You are solely responsible for securing access to your computer and protecting any exported .json or .csv backup files. Unforgettable Management Ltd accepts no responsibility or liability for unauthorized access to your computer or backup files.
